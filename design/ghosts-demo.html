Cursor history

Every visitor's cursor path is saved and replayed in order, counting up from visitor 1 to the total. Over time the paths weave a gold map of where people looked. These visitors are simulated.

How it would work

RecordingWhile someone is on the page, their cursor position is saved about 10 times a second, for up to 30 seconds. On phones, finger drags are saved instead.
StorageEach path is only 2 to 5 KB, kept in the Upstash database we already have. A thousand visitors is a few MB. No new service is needed.
PrivacyPositions only, never who someone is. A one-line notice says paths are saved anonymously, with an opt-out.
Watch outSomeone could use a script to "draw" words with fake cursor paths. Short time limits and dropping unnatural paths cut most of this, but it's the one place policing could come back.
