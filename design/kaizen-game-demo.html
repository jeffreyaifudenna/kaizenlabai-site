Kaizen: a choose-your-path chat game

This is how it would play inside the menu window. It starts with your two steps: “Start Kaizen?” (any answer gets the thank you), then “What does that mean?” with three answers. After that it continues into the three draft paths. All three story directions are playable here so you can compare them. The wording is a first draft for you to change.

The three story directions

A · One PercentA daily quest. You pick an area of life and a size of step, and leave with one tiny action to do today. Gives people a reason to come back each day.
B · The LabYou are handed a problem and choose how to solve it. Each ending points to the matching part of your site: Learning, Agents, Automations or Open Source. Best for showing what KaizenLabAI does.
C · The GardenA quiet zen path with short reflective choices that ends on a single line to keep. Best for mood and brand feel.
