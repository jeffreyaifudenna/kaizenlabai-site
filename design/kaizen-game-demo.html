Kaizen: rough draft 1

A fresh start. One short story in five steps, set in the lab at night. Every choice quietly counts toward one of three endings. Your opening lines are kept: “Start Kaizen?” and the thank you. Everything else is a draft to rewrite.

⋮⋮ MENU KAIZEN

The draft at a glance

Step 1“Start Kaizen?” Yes, No or Maybe later. Any answer: “Thank you for finishing Kaizen's first step.”
Step 2The lab is dark, one lamp is on. Pick up the cracked bowl, the notebook, or the humming machine.
Step 3A note says something here is 1% worse than yesterday. Search, ask, or sit and watch.
Step 4The clock runs a minute slow. Fix it, write down why, or simply arrive a minute early.
Step 5At dawn a shop owner knocks with a problem too big for one day. Build, teach, or find its smallest part.
EndingsThe Maker (points to Agents and Automations), The Teacher (Learning) or The Gardener (Open Source). Each ends with “Thank you for finishing Kaizen's fifth step.”
