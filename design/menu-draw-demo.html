Menu with Draw

Click the bar, then pick Draw. The cursor becomes a gold-tipped pencil and you can draw on the page in gold. Press Esc or Done to stop. "Go to" and "Connect" are removed. On a phone, your finger draws.

What else the menu could have

1 · Save drawingDownloads the page with your gold drawing as an image, with a small KaizenLabAI mark. People share these.
2 · Kaizen of the dayShows one short line about getting a little better each day. A new one daily.
3 · Zen modeHides everything except the logo and your gold lines. Click anywhere to return.
4 · Brush stylesSwitches the pencil between a fine line, an ink brush and the kintsugi crack.
5 · Sound on/offA soft chime when the menu opens and a faint brush sound while drawing. Off by default.
6 · EnsōDraws a gold zen circle around the logo in one slow brush stroke.
7 · Surprise meRuns a random small effect each time, so there is a reason to try it twice.
8 · What's newA short list of the latest changes to the site and channel, in the kaizen spirit.
