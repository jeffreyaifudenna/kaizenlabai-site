Ways to make the whole site more fun (still clean and minimal)

  • The page is the material. Everything you do leaves a quiet mark: cracks mended in gold, ink strokes. Refresh and it is new again. Kintsugi becomes the site's signature.
  • One object, many states. The logo is the only "thing". The remote changes its mood: break, mend, breathe (Zen), sound. Very few elements, a lot of play.
  • Lab instruments. The remote gains small real readouts, like a live clock, bots seen today and visitor count, shown like dials on a device. Fun through data, no decoration.
