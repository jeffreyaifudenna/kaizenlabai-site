Gold shimmer when the window moves

Drag each window by its top bar. Each effect only shows while the window moves, then fades away to calm. All three are light enough for phones.

A · Kintsugi seams

Kintsugi is the art of mending broken pottery with gold. While you drag, fine gold seams run across the window and shimmer, then fade when it rests.
